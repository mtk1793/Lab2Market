# AddManuChain: 60-Second Pitch Script with Scene Directions

---

## PRODUCTION OVERVIEW

**Format:** Mix of YOU (talking head) + animated graphics + data visualizations  
**Duration:** Exactly 60 seconds  
**Camera Setup:** Medium to close-up shots, minimal background, focus on speaker  
**Color Palette:** Industry-specific (O&G: Blue, Aerospace: Silver/White, Defense: Dark Green), final shot: brand colors  
**Tone:** Confident founder + honest about unknowns + energy building to conviction  
**Key Visual Metaphor:** Fragmentation → Connection → Resolution

---

## [0-5 SECONDS] — INTRO + INSIGHT

### SCENE:
- **Camera:** High-energy fade-in. Start on YOU (medium shot, natural lighting, slight smile confidence)
- **Background:** Dark, minimal. Focus on speaker.
- **Graphic Overlay:** Your name + "Founder, AddManuChain" fades in bottom-right (subtle)
- **Transition:** As you say "discovery," the background SHIFTS—three industry visuals flash in sequence behind you:
  - O&G platform (moody blue, industrial)
  - Aerospace factory (clean white, precision)
  - Naval/Defense shipyard (steel gray, complexity)
  - All three MERGE into one unified view
- **Visual Metaphor:** Broken puzzle pieces on each industry image, then they align to show they're THE SAME PIECE repeated

### SCRIPT:
"I'm Mahmoud, building AddManuChain.

We discovered something wild: Supply chains break the exact same way across O&G, aerospace, and defense.

Data everywhere. Connection nowhere. Same problem, three industries."

## [5-15 SECONDS] — PROOF

### SCENE:
- **Camera:** Still on you, but now split-screen effect appears left side
- **Left Side Graphic:** Animated data flows—CAD files, facility specs, quality docs, procurement orders—all moving independently, NOT connecting. Red X's appear where connections should be.
- **Animation Speed:** Moderate, builds tension through repetition
- **Text Overlays (appear sequentially):**
  - "Design? Locked in CAD" (CAD file graphic shows lock icon)
  - "Facilities? Locked with vendors" (vendor portal locks)
  - "Quality? Locked with certifiers" (certification doc locks)
  - "Procurement? Locked away" (supply orders lock)
- **Color Palette:** Cool grays + red accent on the locks
- **Sound:** Subtle "click" sound effect as each lock appears (optional but adds to tension)
- **Visual Conclusion:** All four locked systems freeze on screen, showing the fragmentation problem

### SCRIPT:
"90 conversations confirmed it.

Design? Locked in CAD. Facilities? Locked with vendors. Quality? Locked with certifiers. Procurement? Locked away.

It's not different problems. It's the same architecture failure playing out three times."

## [15-25 SECONDS] — THE COST (Fast + Sharp)

### SCENE:
- **Transition:** Screen fades to black. Only YOU remain visible (tight close-up on face)
- **Three Numbers Appear in Sequence (one per statement):**
  1. **"$100K PER DAY"** appears LARGE (bold white text, 40pt+), followed by subtitle "(Offshore Downtime)"
     - Background graphic: Oil platform at night, frozen time animation
  2. **"40% WASTE"** (red highlight) with subtitle "(Aerospace Inventory Duplication)"
     - Background: Warehouse shelves, inventory bins overflowing
  3. **"$5-11 BILLION"** (biggest number, BOLD RED, emphasis) with subtitle "(Annual Preventable Loss, All Three Sectors)"
     - Background: All three industries merge, $ symbols rain down
- **Animation Effect:** Each number ZOOMS in, lands with slight impact sound
- **Color Transition:** Numbers go white → yellow → RED as figures get bigger
- **Timing:** Leave each number on-screen for 4-5 seconds so it LANDS

### SCRIPT:
"$5-11 billion annually. That's real money from real operators.

Offshore loses $100K per day waiting for parts. Aerospace wastes 40% on duplicate inventory. Defense can't move because supply chains are frozen.

And they all told us this directly."

## [25-40 SECONDS] — WHY NOW (Energy)

### SCENE:
- **Layout:** Three converging arrows/paths animated from three corners of screen toward CENTER
- **Arrow 1 (Left - Red):** Label "Post-COVID Supply Shock" 
  - Background: Supply chain chaos, vendors consolidating, lead times stretching
  - Timeline marker: 2024-2025
- **Arrow 2 (Top - Blue):** Label "AM Adoption Inflection"
  - Background: 3D printers in facilities, dust particles, production happening
  - Timeline marker: 2024-2025
- **Arrow 3 (Right - Green):** Label "Certification Codification"
  - Background: Lloyd's, DNV, CSA logos appear
  - Timeline marker: "RIGHT NOW"
- **Center Point:** All three arrows CONVERGE at center, labeled "2026 WINDOW OPEN"
  - Add subtle pulse/glow effect around center point
- **Energy:** Let the animation BUILD energy as arrows move toward center
- **Final Visual:** Add a timer/clock showing a "closing" animation—window starting to close—adds urgency without desperation

### SCRIPT:
"Here's the beautiful part: Three things aligned in 2026.

Post-COVID supply shock made delays unacceptable. Additive manufacturing went from lab toy to production-ready. Regulators—Lloyd's, DNV, CSA—are right now codifying AM certification.

This window? It closes fast."

## [40-55 SECONDS] — WHAT WE'RE TESTING (Hungry Founder Energy)

### SCENE:
- **Layout:** Three vertical "pilot tracks" appear side-by-side on screen
- **Pilot Track 1 (Left - O&G Color: Blue):**
  - Label: "Q2 2026: O&G Pilot"
  - Question mark icon (pulsing, not static)
  - Graphic: Offshore platform, 24-hour timer, parts moving
  - Text: "Can we deliver certified parts in 24 hours instead of 10 days?"
  
- **Pilot Track 2 (Center - Aerospace Color: Silver):**
  - Label: "Q3 2026: Aerospace Pilot"
  - Question mark icon (pulsing)
  - Graphic: Aircraft, warehouse inventory, 15-20% reduction arrow
  - Text: "Can we cut inventory fragmentation by 15-20%?"
  
- **Pilot Track 3 (Right - Defense Color: Green):**
  - Label: "Q4 2026: Defense Pilot"
  - Question mark icon (pulsing)
  - Graphic: Military vessel, security lock icons, secure network
  - Text: "Supply chain visibility without breaking security?"

- **Animation Progress:** Small progress indicators under each track show "In Progress" state
- **Timeline:** "Q1 2027" appears at bottom with "VERDICT: Real business or interesting experiment?"
- **Tone:** This is HONEST—not overpromising. The question marks should feel real, not desperate.

### SCRIPT:
"We're not claiming we solved it. We're testing if we can.

Q2: O&G pilot—can we deliver certified parts in 24 hours instead of 10 days?

Q3: Aerospace—can we cut inventory fragmentation by 15-20%?

Q4: Defense—supply chain visibility without breaking security.

By Q1 2027: We'll know if this is a real business or a really interesting experiment."

## [55-60 SECONDS] — CLOSE (Conviction)

### SCENE:
- **Transition:** Screen fades to black. Only YOUR face appears in spotlight (tight close-up, direct eye contact with camera)
- **Background:** Dark, minimal, NO distractions
- **Time-lapse Background Animation (subtle, behind you):** 
  - Data streams connecting, walls dissolving, networks lighting up
  - Visualization of "fragmentation becoming irrelevant"
  - All happening QUIETLY behind you—viewer focus stays on YOUR face
- **Final Logo Moment:**
  - As you say "Let's go find out" → AddManuChain logo appears LARGE below your name
  - Logo shows three connected systems (O&G, Aerospace, Defense) unified by central node
  - Logo glows subtly
- **Fade:** Logo holds on-screen for 2 seconds after you finish
- **Super Text (optional):** "AddManuChain.com" or relevant CTA appears bottom right

### SCRIPT:
"We're not another supply chain tool. We're solving information architecture.

Connecting what's broken. Not inventing new stuff.

If this works? Fragmentation becomes irrelevant.

Let's go find out."

## PRODUCTION CHECKLIST FOR DIRECTOR/VIDEOGRAPHER

### Camera & Setup
- [ ] Quiet room (no background noise, no echo)
- [ ] Professional lighting (key light 45°, fill light, backlight soft)
- [ ] Natural eye-level camera position (don't shoot down or up)
- [ ] Good microphone (lavalier or studio mic, test levels first)
- [ ] 4K camera minimum, 24fps or 60fps (test before shooting)

### Graphics Assets Needed
- [ ] Three industry background images (O&G, Aerospace, Defense)
- [ ] Locked system icons (CAD lock, vendor lock, certification lock, procurement lock)
- [ ] Dollar bill animations ($100K, 40%, $5-11B graphics)
- [ ] Three converging arrows (Post-COVID, AM, Certification)
- [ ] Three pilot track layout (Q2, Q3, Q4 with question marks)
- [ ] AddManuChain logo (final shot) with connected network design

### Animation Priorities
1. **Must Have:** Three dollar figures appearing/landing ($100K, 40%, $5-11B)
2. **Should Have:** Industry backgrounds shifting during intro
3. **Should Have:** Three arrows converging for "Why Now" section
4. **Nice to Have:** Locked systems animating during "Proof" section
5. **Nice to Have:** Data streams connecting in background during close

### Audio Requirements
- [ ] Speaker mic (clean, no plosives, moderate level)
- [ ] Optional: Subtle sound effects (lock clicks, number impacts, transitions whoosh)
- [ ] Optional: Royalty-free background music (subtle, underscore only, no dialogue overlap)
- [ ] Final audio mix: Speaker 80%, effects/music 20%

### Editing & Timing
- [ ] Synchronize script read with graphics animations (CRITICAL)
- [ ] Test total runtime with timer—must be exactly 60 seconds
- [ ] Leave 2-second hold on logo at end
- [ ] All transitions should be smooth (no jarring cuts)
- [ ] Color grade: Bright, confident, not washed out

### Acting/Delivery Direction for Speaker
- **Section 1 (0-5s):** Curious, thoughtful opening. Slight lean forward.
- **Section 2 (5-15s):** Analytical, confident. Let the locks appear naturally.
- **Section 3 (15-25s):** Let numbers land. Pause after each figure.
- **Section 4 (25-40s):** Energy building. Lean in slightly on "This window? It closes fast."
- **Section 5 (40-55s):** Honest, matter-of-fact. Own the question marks.
- **Section 6 (55-60s):** Conviction. Direct eye contact. "Let's go find out" is the closing power statement.

### Quality Assurance
- [ ] Run full video 3 times with timer (must be exactly 60 seconds each time)
- [ ] Test on phone speaker (that's how judges will watch)
- [ ] Confirm all graphics are readable on small screen
- [ ] Check for any text that's too small or hard to read
- [ ] Audio levels consistent throughout (no jumps or dips)
- [ ] Color consistency across all scenes

### File Export
- [ ] Format: MP4 (H.264, 1080p or 4K)
- [ ] Codec: H.264 for broad compatibility
- [ ] Frame rate: 24fps or 29.97fps
- [ ] Bitrate: 8-12 Mbps for quality without huge file size
- [ ] Filename: `AddManuChain_Pitch_60sec_FINAL.mp4`

## QUICK CUT GUIDE (If Timing Runs Over)

If total video exceeds 60 seconds, cut in this priority order (lowest to highest impact):
1. Trim closing animations by 0.5 seconds
2. Reduce pause between closing statements
3. Speed up pilot track animations slightly
4. Reduce hold time on dollar figures (but keep all three)
5. Last resort: Cut one pilot track (not recommended, but Q4 Defense is least critical)

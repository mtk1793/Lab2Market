## SHOT BREAKDOWN (Scene-by-Scene)

### SHOT 1: INTRO (0-5 seconds)

**Setup:**
- YOU sit facing camera, medium shot
- Name/title graphic fades in bottom-right
- Background: dark, minimal

**Action:**
- Start speaking confidently
- At "discovery" → background SHIFTS
- Three industry images flash: O&G (blue) → Aerospace (white) → Defense (gray)
- All three merge and align

**Key Frame:** Three industries unified behind you, puzzle pieces click together

### SHOT 2: PROOF (5-15 seconds)

**Setup:**
- YOU still visible, now with split-screen effect
- Left side: Animated locked systems appear

**Action:**
- As you speak each system, lock icon appears with subtle "click" sound
- "Design? Locked in CAD" → CAD lock ⬜
- "Facilities? Locked with vendors" → Vendor lock ⬜
- "Quality? Locked with certifiers" → Cert lock ⬜
- "Procurement? Locked away" → Procurement lock ⬜
- All four locks freeze, showing fragmentation

**Visual Metaphor:** Each lock = broken connection point

**Visual Metaphor:** Numbers get BIGGER as cost increases

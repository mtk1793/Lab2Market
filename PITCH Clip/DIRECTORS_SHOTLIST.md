## TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

**Camera:**
- 4K or 1080p minimum
- 24fps or 60fps
- Tripod or stable mount

**Lighting:**
- Key light: 45° angle, moderate intensity
- Fill light: Opposite side, lower intensity (3:1 ratio)
- Backlight: Behind speaker, soft glow (creates separation)
- Overall: Bright but not washed out, professional look

**Audio:**
- Lavalier mic or studio mic (wireless if possible)
- Pop filter on mic (no plosives)
- Monitor levels in real-time
- Quiet room (no HVAC, traffic, echoing)

**Graphics/Post-Production:**
- All animations pre-made and timed to script
- Sync graphics to speaker delivery
- Sound design (lock clicks, number impacts, arrow whooshes, connection pulse)
- Color grade (bright, confident, brand-consistent)

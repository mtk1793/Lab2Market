## The Three Critical Blockers IRAP Identified

### **1. LIABILITY & WARRANTY (The Elephant)**
**The Problem:**
When a 3D-printed part fails in operational use, WHO is liable?
- The OEM (owns design)?
- The printing facility (operator error)?
- The material supplier?
- AddManuChain?
- The end-user?

**Your Current Answer:** "Still figuring it out. People have different ideas about that."

**IRAP's Response:** This is **non-negotiable** for market entry, especially defense/aerospace. You cannot proceed to paying customers without clear liability assignment.

**The Business Impact:**
- Defense procurement teams won't sign contracts (liability undefined = too risky)
- Insurance companies won't insure your platform
- Customers will demand indemnification
- **This is a blocker, not a nice-to-have**

**What to Do:**
- Hire aerospace/defense-experienced legal counsel ASAP
- Define clear liability chain: pre-certification → process control → testing → liability assignment
- Model: If all pre-certified, process controlled, and tested, then YOU (AddManuChain) bear liability IF defect is traced to platform failure

### **2. QUALITY ASSURANCE (Per-Part Guarantee)**
**The Problem:**
You said: "We batch-test samples, guarantee 98-99% quality"

IRAP asked: "Defense/aerospace requires 99.97%+ quality. What about the 1-2% failures?"

**Your Answer:** "We have testing labs, non-destructive + destructive testing, multiple quality layers."

**IRAP's Concern:**
- Batch testing = statistical assurance of population, NOT individual part guarantee
- Mission-critical parts require individual per-print verification
- Can't achieve 99.97%+ with batch testing alone
- Need real-time monitoring, AI-powered defect detection, per-print inspection

**What to Do:**
- Invest in real-time quality tech (inline inspection, AI defect detection)
- Move from batch testing (population assurance) to per-print verification (individual assurance)
- Partner with quality-tech vendors (NDT scanning, vision systems)
- This is a Phase 2-3 innovation project (not immediate, but on roadmap)

### **3. OEM PARTNERSHIP VIABILITY (The Business Model Test)**
**The Problem:**
You're asking OEMs to:
1. Share/license digital designs (break traditional IP secrecy)
2. Abandon aftermarket spare parts revenue (40-60% margin historically)
3. Adopt royalty-based per-print licensing (uncertain, unpredictable)
4. Trust your platform with their most valuable IP

**Your Evidence:** "60+ companies talked to. Galaxy and Reaction Dynamics support the idea."

**IRAP's Question:** "Support the idea" ≠ "Will bet their business on your platform." Do you have signed commitments?

**Your Answer:** "Planning meetings to convince them to accept pilot programs."

**IRAP's Take:** You're building a platform assuming OEM participation that hasn't been formalized. This is high-risk.

**What to Do:**
- Secure at least 1 signed LOI or pilot agreement from an OEM
- Define exact terms: IP licensing approach, per-print royalty %, testing requirements
- Get OEM explicitly committing to digital licensing model
- Use this as foundation before scaling platform

## What IRAP Is NOT

❌ **Not a Series A replacement.**  IRAP is ~$50-300K over 12-24 months. VC Series A is $1-5M+. IRAP helps you get investor-ready.

❌ **Not an operational funding source.** IRAP funds innovation labor costs, NOT regular software development or salaries. You must fund operations yourself.

❌ **Not a quick process.** Relationship-based, multiple meetings required, no hard decision timeline. Expect 2-3 months to get Phase 1 project approved.

❌ **Not a guarantee.** IRAP can say "no" if strategic fit isn't right or execution risk is too high.

## What IRAP Is (The 80% Value Proposition)

✅ **Innovation Technical Advisors (ITAs)**
Expert consultants matched to your specific innovation challenges (quality tech, liability, regulatory). They guide you through de-risking.

✅ **Regulatory & Standards Expertise**
Help you navigate DNV, ASME, defense certification pathways. Connections to certification bodies.

✅ **Market & Business Guidance**
Help refine commercialization strategy, business model, competitive positioning.

✅ **Network & Introductions**
Connections to government buyers (DND, etc.), service providers, testing labs, customers.

✅ **Credibility**
IRAP backing = validation signal to investors, customers, partners. "NRC-supported company" carries weight in Canada.

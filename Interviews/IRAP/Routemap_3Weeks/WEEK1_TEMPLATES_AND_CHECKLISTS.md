## TEMPLATE 4: INTERNAL ALIGNMENT MEETING (WEEK 1, DAY 1)

**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Attendees:** Mahmoud (CEO), Co-founder (Product), Intern (Note-taker)

### **Agenda**

**Section 1: Liability Framework (20 min)**
- What is our current understanding of liability assignment?
- Where are we most vulnerable?
- What questions do we NEED legal counsel to answer?
- Decision: Proceed with external legal counsel? (Yes/No/Maybe)

**Section 2: OEM Partnership (20 min)**
- Who are our top 2-3 OEM targets?
- Why would they actually do this (beyond "it's a good idea")?
- What's the biggest objection they'll raise? How do we answer it?
- Decision: Which OEM to target first?

**Section 3: Pilot Definition (15 min)**
- What part should we pilot?
- Which customer would be best partner?
- What's the realistic 4-6 month timeline?
- Decision: Part + Customer confirmed?

**Section 4: IRAP Communication (5 min)**
- When do we send thank-you email?
- What do we commit to in terms of timeline/updates?
- Decision: Send Week 1 Day 1? (Yes/No)

- OEM: [Top 2 targets] + [Why they'd adopt]

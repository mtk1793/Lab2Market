## IRAP 101 (What You Need to Know)

### **Who They Are**
- Federal government program (National Research Council)
- Goal: Help Canadian companies grow through innovation
- Budget: Project-based, 12-24 month engagements
- Relationship-based (NOT transactional form-filling)

### **What They Fund**
✅ **YES:**
- Labor costs for innovation projects (40-60% of cost)
- R&D de-risking (tech, market, regulatory)
- Innovation Technical Advisors (experts matched to your needs)

❌ **NO:**
- Operational software development
- Regular salaries/headcount
- Non-labor costs (you fund materials, equipment)
- Business as usual work

### **What They Expect to See**
- **Traction:** Early customers, your own funding, market validation
- **Growth:** Clear path to scale with competitive differentiation
- **Innovation:** Ability to overcome specific technical/market/regulatory blockers
- **Team:** Capable to execute (may have gaps, but path to fill them)
- **Commitment:** Skin in the game (you must co-fund)

### **TRL Levels They Support**
- **TRL 3-4:** Proof of concept (you start here)
- **TRL 5-6:** Prototype with field data (target for now)
- **TRL 7-8:** Pilot/commercial deployment (goal for IRAP support)

They help you climb the ladder; they don't jump multiple levels.

## THE THREE BLOCKERS

### **Blocker #1: Undefined Liability**
**Problem:** When a 3D-printed part fails in the field, who pays?
- OEM? Manufacturing facility? Material supplier? You?

**Why It's Blocking:**
- Defense procurement won't sign contracts without clear liability
- Insurance companies won't insure your platform
- Operators won't adopt without indemnification

**What You Said:** "Still figuring it out. Different people have different ideas."  
**What IRAP Heard:** "You haven't solved this. This could be a catastrophic business risk."

**The Fix:**
Hire aerospace/defense-experienced legal counsel. Create clear liability assignment model BEFORE pursuing customers.

### **Blocker #2: Unvalidated OEM Partnership Model**
**Problem:** You're assuming OEMs will pivot from aftermarket spare parts (40-60% margin) to royalty-based digital licensing (uncertain margin).

**Why It's Blocking:**
- This requires fundamental business model change for OEMs
- They have no incentive to participate if margins shrink
- You're building platform assuming OEM participation that may not materialize

**What You Said:** "Talking to 60+ companies. Galaxy and Reaction Dynamics 'support the idea.'"  
**What IRAP Heard:** "You have interest but no committed partners. You're building without them."

**The Fix:**
Secure signed LOI or pilot agreement from at least 1 OEM BEFORE heavy platform investment. Get explicit commitment to digital licensing approach.

### **Blocker #3: Missing Pilot Validation**
**Problem:** All your insights come from interviews, not operational data.

**Why It's Blocking:**
- "Customers like the idea" ≠ "customers will use it and pay for it"
- Operational reality creates unexpected constraints
- You don't know if your platform actually solves the real problem

**What You Said:** "Planning meetings to convince them to accept pilot."  
**What IRAP Heard:** "You don't have a pilot yet. You're pre-revenue at core product."

**The Fix:**
Execute a pilot with 1 OEM + 1 part user on 1 specific part. Document: lead time, cost, quality, satisfaction. Use results to refine model.

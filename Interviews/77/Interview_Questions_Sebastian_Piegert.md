# Interview 77: Sebastian Piegert — Interview Questions
**Siemens Energy, Department Lead Additive Manufacturing**

---

## 5 Main Questions

### 1. **Factory Floor Reality: What's the Gap Between AM Strategy & Production?**
You and Lisa are presenting at the AM Forum on "factory head's reality check" — what are the top 3 obstacles you've encountered scaling additive manufacturing from R&D into production-grade turbine manufacturing at scale?

### 2. **Certification & Supply Chain: How Do You Handle Downtime Today?**
When a critical turbine component fails at a remote or offshore customer location, what's your current fastest path to get a certified replacement part? What's the typical lead time, and how much does downtime cost the customer?

### 3. **The 90% Rule: What % of Your Parts Are Actually AM-Viable?**
Not all turbine parts are ideal for AM. In your view, what percentage of Siemens Energy's installed base of turbine components *could realistically* be 3D-printed while meeting DNV/Lloyd's certification? What's the strategy for selecting those parts?

### 4. **On-Demand Certified Parts: Would a Distributed Model Work?**
Imagine a global network where OEMs like Siemens Energy deposit certified CAD designs, and regional print centers produce on-demand certified parts. What would need to be true for Siemens Energy to (a) share designs, and (b) trust a third-party network to print your certified spares?

### 5. **Competitive Threat or Enabler: What's Your Take on Distributed Manufacturing?**
Many OEMs see distributed 3D printing as a threat to their service revenue. How does Siemens Energy view the shift toward on-demand, on-location manufacturing? Is it a business threat, a customer retention tool, or something else?

---

## 2 Bonus Questions

### B1. **Regional Expansion: Winston-Salem & Beyond**
Your Winston-Salem Technology Center is freshly announced as a one-stop shop with conventional machining, welding, and advanced inspection. Is this Siemens Energy positioning to offer AM services *directly to customers*, or enabling your OEM partners to do so?

### B2. **AddManuChain Platform Fit: Would You Play?**
If we showed you a platform that lets you control the entire workflow (design governance → regional printing → audit trail → delivery), would Siemens Energy consider participating as:
  - **(a)** An OEM partner depositing designs & earning royalties?
  - **(b)** A print center operator in North America?
  - **(c)** Neither, and if not, why?

---

## Interview Flow

1. **Warm-up (2 min):** Acknowledge his role, recent moves, and the AM Forum presentation
2. **Core discussion (40 min):** Work through Q1–Q5 in order, letting him expand on factory-floor constraints
3. **Strategic fit (8 min):** Bonus questions to gauge Siemens Energy's openness to AddManuChain model
4. **Close (5 min):** Thank him, confirm next steps, ask if he'd like to see platform demo

---

## Notes
- **Tone:** Technical, practical, reality-focused (skip marketing language)
- **Leverage:** He's in the midst of proving AM works at scale—ask him what has and hasn't worked
- **OEM Mindset:** Siemens Energy earns service revenue from spares; ask if distributed manufacturing changes that calculus
- **Listen for:** Certification workflows, inventory optimization wins, customer demand signals

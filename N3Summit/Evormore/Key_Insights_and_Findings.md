# Evormore - Key Insights and Findings

## Company Overview
**Evormore** is an AI enablement and training organization that evolved from their origins as a traditional Silicon Valley startup. They recognized that AI success is fundamentally about **people, not products or technology**.

---

## Core Business Model

### Service Offerings
1. **Strategic Intelligence**: Help organizations develop a clear North Star and strategic direction for AI implementation
2. **Workforce Intelligence**: Train frontline workers and staff on AI tools and processes
3. **Data Intelligence**: Set up databases and systems appropriately to unlock company data value

### Key Philosophy
- **Data is the unlock**: Data collection, management, process management, and analysis are the true enablers of AI
- **Reject restrictive policies**: Challenge the "don't use company data" mindset - this misses the entire point of AI
- **Ownership matters**: Customers should own their infrastructure as an asset on their balance sheet

## Flagship Use Case: Meat & Poultry Ontario

### Problem Statement
- Recall events cost minimum $30,000+ (up to $100,000+) if critical control points aren't properly documented
- Hard to train operators on complex compliance procedures
- Government regulations require specific temperatures, temps, nitrate checks, cooking times, etc.

### Solution Delivered
**AI-enabled process guidance system** with automatic compliance reporting:

1. **Operator Interface**: Operator enters "new batch" and product selection
2. **Guided Process**: AI guides operator through each step of the manufacturing process
3. **Automated Data Capture**: System passively captures all data into SQL database
4. **Real-Time Compliance**: Tracks all critical control points automatically
5. **Auto-Generated Reports**: At completion, system generates compliance report including:
   - Batch ID and completion status
   - All ingredients used
   - All critical control points touched
   - Instruments used and their calibration status
   - Corrective actions taken (if any)
   - Cooling procedures followed

### Key Benefits
- **Reduces compliance risk**: Eliminates documentation gaps
- **Trains by doing**: Frontline worker doesn't need extensive training
- **Zero additional burden**: Operator just follows the process naturally
- **Complete audit trail**: Full chain of custody automatically maintained

## Market Insights & Challenges

### Adoption Gap
- **Only ~1% of manufacturers** in Canada have a clearly articulated AI goal
- Remaining 99% experience **analysis paralysis**
- Great minds tell them they need to move forward, but trusted advisors push them toward Microsoft/vendor lock-in instead

### Defense Industry Specific Issues
- Analysis paralysis is widespread in defense sector
- Leaders defaulting to "buy ChatGPT for everyone" and "check the boxes"
- Restrictive policies on company data usage limit AI effectiveness to single-player productivity (email writing)
- This approach **never creates business value that "trickles up"**
- Controlled goods data (ITAR, etc.) from suppliers/vendors creates additional complexity

### Leadership Gap
- Most defense leaders lack understanding of how to satisfy DoD data requirements while implementing AI
- Trusted advisors are vendors pushing their own solutions rather than strategic advisors

## Data Sovereignty & Control

### Why Sovereignty Matters
- **2018 US Cloud Act**: US government can access data stored on US company servers, regardless of physical location
- Evormore prioritizes **chain of custody** for company data
- Partners specifically choose non-US controlled infrastructure (OVH Cloud from France)

### Evormore's Approach
- Set up private servers in Canada with sovereign infrastructure
- Customers own their infrastructure as a company asset
- Complete control over data flow and API endpoints
- No "black box" reliance on vendor platforms

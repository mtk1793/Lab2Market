# Evormore: Key Insights & Strategic Analysis

**Metadata**: Date Context: 2026 | Evormore (Services Company) | Topic: AI Implementation for Manufacturing with Data Sovereignty Focus

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Evormore is a services company that helps manufacturers implement AI solutions with a strong emphasis on data sovereignty, open source technologies, and practical workforce training. They focus on unlocking the value of company data rather than just providing access to public AI tools, helping companies set up private AI infrastructure that they own and control.

## Technical Insights
- Evormore uses OVH Cloud (French company) for sovereign AI infrastructure, avoiding US-controlled systems like Microsoft Azure
- They implement open source solutions including Open Web UI and Easy Panel for container management
- Their approach connects AI directly to SQL databases for process guidance and automated reporting
- They utilize large parameter models (7B to 120B) through API endpoints with unlimited usage
- The system creates automated reports including critical control points, ingredient scaling, and process documentation

## Market Positioning
- Positions itself as an enablement company rather than just a technology vendor
- Focuses on training frontline workers and helping companies develop AI North Star strategies
- Addresses the gap where companies buy AI tools (like ChatGPT licenses) but don't use company data due to policy restrictions
- Serves manufacturers across Canada through partnership with Excellence in Manufacturing Consortium (1500 companies)
- Targets regulated industries like meat processing where data traceability is critical for compliance

## Strategic Implications for AddManuChain
- Demonstrates successful model of sovereign AI infrastructure using non-US cloud providers
- Shows value proposition of combining open source tools with proprietary training methodologies
- Highlights importance of data ownership and control in manufacturing AI applications
- Provides blueprint for helping SMEs implement AI without ongoing vendor lock-in or subscription costs
- Illustrates how AI can be integrated with existing manufacturing processes for compliance and quality control

## Challenges Identified
- Only ~1% of Canadian manufacturers have clearly articulated AI business goals
- Defense industry faces analysis paralysis despite knowing they need AI implementation
- Trusted advisors often push US-controlled solutions (Microsoft) that don't meet sovereignty requirements
- Companies struggle to move beyond single-user productivity tools to enterprise-wide data utilization
- Cost concerns prevent adoption despite available open source alternatives

## Recommendations
1. Partner with sovereign cloud providers (like OVH) to offer AddManuChain users guaranteed data residency options
2. Develop training programs that help manufacturers articulate clear AI business objectives and North Star strategies
3. Create pre-configured open source AI stacks that manufacturers can deploy and own outright
4. Build industry-specific AI templates (starting with food processing) that automate compliance reporting
5. Establish a manufacturer peer network to share sovereign AI implementation best practices
6. Create cost comparison models showing long-term savings of open source vs. proprietary AI solutions

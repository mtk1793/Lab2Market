# Maya Panel 2: Key Insights & Strategic Analysis

**Metadata**: Date Context: 2026 | Maya (Industrial AI Company) | Topic: Practical AI Applications in Manufacturing and Industrial Operations

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Maya is an established industrial AI company with 40 years of experience in manufacturing and engineering. They provide practical AI solutions focused on business outcomes rather than technology for technology's sake. Their approach centers on the EPIC framework (Energy, Productivity, Inventory, Control, and Assistant technologies) to address real manufacturing challenges with measurable ROI.

## Technical Insights
- Maya's EPIC framework covers 5 key areas: Energy (forecasting, switching), Productivity (OEE tracking, control optimization), Inventory (supply chain forecasting, quality inspection), Control (predictive maintenance, process control), and Assistant (document/chatbots, computer vision)
- They've deployed over 200 AI projects across manufacturing sites
- Solutions include predictive maintenance using degradation data, computer vision for quality inspection, reinforcement learning for process control, and NLP for document processing
- Emphasis on grounding AI in physics and business values, not just statistical accuracy
- Focus on usable data frequency, quality, and accessibility rather than collecting all available data

## Market Positioning
- Positions as a practical AI partner for manufacturers, not a technology vendor
- 40-year history in manufacturing provides deep domain expertise
- Focus on business outcomes: waste reduction, productivity increases, cost savings
- Solutions designed for shop floor implementation with technician acceptance
- Emphasis on change management and stakeholder engagement (executive sponsors and shop floor champions)
- Proven track record with measurable ROI (e.g., 420k annual raw material savings, 60 months without failures)

## Strategic Implications for AddManuChain
- Demonstrates successful framework for practical AI implementation in manufacturing (EPIC model)
- Shows importance of business-outcome focus over technology-first approaches
- Highlights value of domain expertise combined with AI capabilities
- Provides template for measuring and communicating AI ROI in manufacturing contexts
- Illustrates effective change management strategies for AI adoption on shop floors

## Challenges Identified
- Need for business executives to champion AI initiatives with budget authority
- Requires shop floor champions to drive operational adoption
- Data quality and accessibility issues (wrong frequency, missing sensors, poor data)
- Change management resistance from experienced operators
- Balancing AI innovation with proven control systems (PID loops)
- Ensuring AI models don't break fundamental engineering/physics rules
- Freedom to operate concerns regarding IP and deployment boundaries

## Recommendations
1. Adopt or adapt Maya's EPIC framework for AddManuChain's manufacturing AI solutions
2. Develop business-case focused AI solutions with clear ROI metrics (waste reduction, productivity gains, cost savings)
3. Create implementation methodologies that include both executive sponsorship and shop floor champion engagement
4. Build data readiness assessment tools to evaluate frequency, quality, and accessibility before AI deployment
5. Develop guardrail mechanisms to ensure AI recommendations respect engineering/physics constraints
6. Create change management programs specifically for manufacturing AI adoption
7. Establish clear boundaries for AI deployment to maintain freedom to operate and IP protections

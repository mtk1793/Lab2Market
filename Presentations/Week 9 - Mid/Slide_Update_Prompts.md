Stage 4 (white box):
Bold: "Stage 4: Procurement / Budget"
Who: VP Operations, CFO
Gate: Annual budget cycle alignment.

Stage 5 (white box):
Bold: "Stage 5: Operational Deployment"

Insight (italic): "Platform must operate as supplier-of-record with contractual SLA penalties, not just as a software tool. Governance must be neutral."

BOTTOM — full-width dark charcoal banner box:
Bold title: "FOUNDATIONAL STAKEHOLDER: THE OEM"
Body: "They control IP. Can block sharing or willing license to outsource non-core service. Obsolete parts = lowest friction entry point."
Second line (new addition): "Secondary foundational risk: Supplier communication failure. Suppliers hide schedule delays (fear of penalties) until cascading delay costs become unavoidable. Platform must surface early warning signals. (Ref: Jared Newcombe — $8M contract termination, Interview #65)"

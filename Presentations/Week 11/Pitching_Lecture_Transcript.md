## KEY INSIGHTS AND TAKEAWAYS

### Core Framework: The 5 Questions Before Any Pitch
1. **Why** are you creating this pitch? (What outcome are you after?)
2. **Who** are you pitching to? (Their perspective, knowledge level, goals)
3. **When** in your business lifecycle are you pitching? (Early stage → show traction metrics: interviews, pilots, LOIs — NOT revenue)
4. **What** is your call to action? (Be explicit — advisors, funding, pilot partners)
5. **How** are you pitching? (Stage, Zoom, boardroom, elevator — format changes preparation)

### The Attunement Principle
- Most pitches fail because founders are **too close to their own technology** — "married to their idea"
- The audience is almost never your technical peer — pitch to the grandma standard
- Below the surface (iceberg model): beliefs, priorities, real pains, risk tolerance — this is where human engagement happens
- Investors/partners are **betting on the founder** as much as the idea

### Story Structure (3-Act Formula)
| Act | Content | What to Show |
|---|---|---|
| **Act 1 — Setup** | Protagonist, goal, obstacle | Who faces the problem, what they want, what blocks them |
| **Act 2 — Discovery** | Trials, revelation, sacrifice | Your solution, business model, competition, "price to pay" |
| **Act 3 — Resolution** | Home, change, inspiration | Future vision, roadmap, call to action |

### Problem Hook: 2 Types
| Type | Approach | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **Micro Hook** | Personal story — a human experience with the problem | Emotional connection, consumer products, founder story |
| **Macro Hook** | Scale statistics — the size and scope of the problem | B2B, industrial, infrastructure — where numbers are the argument |

**For AddManuChain:** Both work — lead with micro ("in the time it took you to listen to this sentence, an offshore platform just lost $50,000") then validate with macro ($500B fragmented supply chain cost).

### What Investors/Funders Look For at Early Stage
- Number of customer interviews conducted
- Letters of intent or pilot signups
- Evidence of market understanding — NOT revenue
- NRC IRAP, Mitacs = **relationship-based** — they're betting on you the person

### Pitching Pitfalls to Avoid
- Too technical — jargon that excludes your audience
- No clear call to action at the end
- Not knowing your numbers cold
- Going over time
- Not being yourself
- Telling a pitch, not a story

### Guy Kawasaki's Core Pitch Principle
> *"You need to make people dream the same dream that you do."*

### Key Challenge: IP Ownership & Ecosystem Trust
**Critical Finding:** IP ownership and liability concerns are THE biggest barrier to ecosystem adoption
- **The Core Problem:** OEM partners are skeptical about sharing IP within an ecosystem platform
  - Not just data security/hacking concerns
  - **Liability question:** "What happens if we share [IP] with you? Who is responsible if something leaks?"
  - This is blocking buy-in from major players despite interest in the solution
- **Why it matters:** In an ecosystems play, you need to convince OEMs to participate and share data/IP - but they're deeply protective
- **Testing approach:** Planning to attend IP lawyer event in Halifax (March 16) to understand legal frameworks for IP sharing within platforms
